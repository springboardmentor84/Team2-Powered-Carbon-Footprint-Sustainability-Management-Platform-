HOW YOUR PROGRESS IS CALCULATED

Progress = (Current Carbon ÷ Target Carbon) × 100

Example: {{ getCurrentCarbon(selectedGoal) | number:'1.0-1' }} ÷ {{ getTargetCarbon(selectedGoal) | number:'1.0-1' }} × 100 = {{ (getCurrentCarbon(selectedGoal) / (getTargetCarbon(selectedGoal) || 1) * 100) | number:'1.0-1' }}%

Note: Displayed progress is capped at 100% once the target has been reached.

Current Carbon: Total carbon emissions from activities counted within this goal's applicable period.

Remaining: Target − current carbon, never below 0.
