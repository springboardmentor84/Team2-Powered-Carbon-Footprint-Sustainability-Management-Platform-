Calculation Metrics

Understand how your carbon footprint and activity metrics are calculated.

calculate Individual Activity
Quantity × Emission Factor
= Carbon Emission

Example: Carbon emission for each activity is calculated from its quantity and the emission factor associated with that selected activity/category.

functions Total Emissions
Σ Individual Carbon Emissions
= Total Emissions

Example: Summing all your recorded activities results in {{ totalCarbon | number:'1.0-2' }} kg CO₂.

analytics Average Carbon Emission
Total Emissions ÷ Activities
= Average / Activity
